What happens on failure or cancellation?
Plain LLM final textReturn incomplete; do not infer a successful structured result.
Helper limit / errorReturn exhausted/error evidence to the active parent. No automatic context repair.
Parent cancelledAbort helper model/tools, await idle, and do not restart Jev. Tools must honor abort.
Restart after interruptionMark the unfinished attempt as an error; preserve pending call identity. No replay.
